# Content analysis

Before this source map, complete the orientation stage in [domain-and-outline.md](domain-and-outline.md). Do not propose definite deletion, shortening, deduplication, or reordering until the theme, professional domain, protected facts, and initial outline have been recorded.

## Build the source map first

Read or listen end to end before optimizing isolated lines. Divide the material by topic change, question change, argumentative step, example, conclusion, and call to action. Preserve the original order in the source map even when a later structure may differ.

For each semantic unit record:

- who speaks and the functional role;
- the claim, question, answer, setup, example, qualification, or conclusion;
- information added compared with prior units;
- dependencies on earlier context;
- timing confidence and transcription uncertainty;
- protected facts such as numbers, units, conditions, negation, comparison, and causality.

## Value and redundancy

Judge information meaning, not string similarity. Two differently worded units may be redundant; two similar units may serve different functions.

Prefer retaining:

- the clearest complete formulation of a core point;
- evidence, examples, or reasoning needed to support it;
- memorable wording that adds communicative value;
- necessary setup, subject, contrast, qualification, or conclusion;
- a second speaker's genuinely new information.

Candidates for removal include:

- clear digressions outside scope;
- failed attempts fully replaced by a complete take;
- repeated explanation with no added premise, nuance, emotion, or evidence;
- host setup that pre-answers a guest whose later answer states the same point more completely;
- empty meta-commentary that neither frames nor advances the content.

## Restructuring

Choose structure from the material: chronology, topic, question/answer, problem/reason/solution, conclusion-first, tutorial steps, or another source-supported form. Do not force a fixed template.

Move complete semantic units. After reordering, inspect every connector, pronoun, reference, time cue, condition, and causal statement. A reordered sequence must remain truthful and speakable without invented narration.

## Source audit

Before approving Pass 1, compare the proposed structure to the full source and ask:

- Is every retained answer paired with enough question or setup?
- Did any deletion remove a premise or condition?
- Has a technical value lost its unit, object, range, or uncertainty?
- Did a clarification get mistaken for repetition?
- Did reordering reverse cause and effect or chronology?
- Does each highlight remain understandable on its own?
